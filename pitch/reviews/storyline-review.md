# PromptCode pitch review

Reviewed September 9, 2026. Scope: all slide content, ordering, claims, and spoken flow. This was a content and structure review, not a fresh visual browser inspection.

## Narrative

Landing hook → hiring risk → why now → solution → product evidence → buyer → customer value → market → differentiation → revenue → acquisition → financial growth → expansion → defensibility → funding milestone → closing.

The revised sequence answers the judges' questions in order: Why care? What works differently? Who buys? What is it worth? Can this become a scalable business? What will the award prove?

The official Maroon description emphasizes fast scaling and venture value. The public page does not provide a scored judging rubric, an eight-minute limit, or a fixed award amount: https://mcferrin.tamu.edu/program/good-bull-pitch/

## Delivery

The refreshed spoken script is about 930 words. Slide timing targets total 7:35 including the landing, leaving 25 seconds within the user's eight-minute ceiling. This is a rehearsal plan, not a measured presentation time. Detailed arithmetic and source qualifications are labeled as supporting detail in presenter-notes.md, not as part of the spoken script.

On the illustration slide, let judges first see the equal clean scores, then reveal the evidence. Explain the difference before advancing. Use the first buyer and pilot as the concrete example that connects product, pricing, acquisition and growth.

## Remaining limitations that wording cannot solve

- No verified paid customers, employer interviews, measured time savings or hiring-outcome improvements were supplied. One real pilot or attributable buyer interview would strengthen the pitch more than additional generic statistics.
- The $53K exposure, $477 capacity value, modeled SAM and financial forecasts answer different questions. Do not combine them into an implied total ROI or present them as achieved savings.
- The ask specifies allocation and milestones but no dollar amount. Use the actual award amount or a justified budget when known; do not invent a competition cap.
- The user requested removal of the team slide. Founder identity remains on the close; relevant founder qualifications can be addressed in Q&A if supplied and verifiable.
- The current site has 15 counted slides plus the landing. PDF and PowerPoint have not been regenerated and do not reflect these revisions.
